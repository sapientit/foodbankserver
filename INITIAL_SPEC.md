#Food Bank System
This system is for the management of a food bank. The food bank runs multiple sessions every week. People form authorised organisations, or authorised people, can refer people to the food bank. This referral includes the session they are attending, whether they need a delivery, number of adults and children, reason for referral, name, address, phone number and food preferences.
The administrators can move people between sessions and cancel people.
Each session generates a food pick list - one "parcel" for each person. The parcel contents depends on the size of the household being supported.
The pick list can then be modified before being printed. Once printed, further changes can be made before the pick-list is confirmed as created. This will update stocks of the different items.
Each session is confirmed by the team lead. For each person, did they attend. If they didn't the parcel is sent back to stock.
